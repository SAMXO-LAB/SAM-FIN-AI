## Full description (max 4000)
Finance Book AI is one calm place for your everyday money: what came in, what went out, what you owe and what you are saving for.

Made for India: rupees by default, lakh/crore formatting, UPI and EMI-friendly.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Track income and expenses with categories, notes, tags and payment method
• Keep several accounts (bank, savings, cash, wallet, credit card) and see your net worth
• Add loans and EMIs and get the full repayment schedule, the interest still to pay and the next due date
• See what happens if you prepay a loan
• Note money you lent or borrowed, with due dates and part payments
• Set monthly budgets by category and see how much you can spend per day
• Create savings goals with a suggested monthly amount
• Attach receipts or photos to transactions (optional)
• Ask Sam, the built-in AI helper, plain questions like "How much did I spend on food this month?" or "When does my bike loan end?"
• Download your data any time as CSV or JSON

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Finance Book AI never asks for your bank login, card number, PIN or OTP
• It does not read your SMS and does not connect to your bank
• Your records are visible only to you
• No ads. No selling your data.
• Delete your account and all data yourself from Settings

Finance Book AI is a tracking and planning tool. It does not give loans, move money or provide financial advice. Sam can make mistakes, so check important figures.

Developed by Chandra Shekar.
